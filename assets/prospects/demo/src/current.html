About Harbourline Plumbing

Harbourline Plumbing Ltd is a family owned and operated plumbing company that has been servicing the greater Auckland region since 2004. We pride ourselves on providing a quality service to all of our customers and we specialise in a wide range of plumbing services for both residential and commercial clients. Our team of qualified plumbers have many years of experience in the industry.

We offer services including but not limited to blocked drains, hot water cylinders, leak detection, bathroom renovations, gas fitting, roofing and spouting, backflow testing and general maintenance. No job is too big or too small. All of our work is fully guaranteed and we are members of the Master Plumbers association.

Please feel free to browse our website to find out more about the services we provide. If you have any questions please do not hesitate to get in touch with us via our contact page. Contact us
