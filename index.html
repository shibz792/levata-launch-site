02 — The problem
The cost of standing still

Most small business websites are quietly losing customers.

A weak website does not announce itself as a problem. It just sits there every day, sending the wrong first impression to the people who were ready to buy.

Slow, and it shows

Clunky, unoptimized builds test the patience of a visitor who has three other tabs open. Every extra second costs you a share of them.

Invisible to Google, and to AI

No real SEO foundation and nothing structured for generative search means you are absent from both the search results and the AI answers replacing them.

It undersells the business

The work is excellent. The website does not say so. Visitors judge credibility from design before they read a single word of copy.

Broken on the device that matters most

Most local searches happen on a phone. A layout that only works on desktop turns away most of the people who find you.

No proof, no trust

Reviews, credentials, and real work are buried or missing, leaving a visitor to guess whether you are actually the right business to call.

Expensive to fix properly, or so it seems

A real custom rebuild has traditionally meant a five figure invoice and months of back and forth. That price tag keeps good businesses stuck.
